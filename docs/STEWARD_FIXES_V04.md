# Steward concern 3 — sponsor-funded transfer recovery

MilestoneBond v0.4 is a new StudioNet contract, not a mutation of v0.3. Its [source](../contracts/milestone_bond_v0_4.py), [deployment record](../deployments/studionet_v0.4.json), and [contract explorer page](https://explorer-studio.genlayer.com/contracts/0x9218b57964ae4eBe9c9756bb48b3454a03Ce707D) identify the release. The deployed source SHA-256 is `e00bc5735a3e5f94e275283fef70d884ca815b0055e9c42d553e13b3e54c1ddc`.

For each project with milestone budget `X`, the sponsor deposits `2X`: `X` escrow plus a separate `X` recovery reserve. Credits are indexed by project. A withdrawal moves only the originating project's credit into a single pending native transfer and binds reconciliation to its exact finalized parent, child and request ID. A confirmed uncredited child restores the claimant's credit only after calculating the actual balance shortfall, which is charged solely to that project's still-locked reserve. If the reserve is insufficient, the claim remains pending until a donated top-up fills the gap. A missing or ambiguous receipt never starts a duplicate payment. The [reserve design](V04_RESERVE_DESIGN.md) states the full accounting invariants and finite coverage limit.

The sponsor can release unused reserve exactly once after project completion/refund and after **all project claims are paid and reconciled**. It becomes sponsor credit, then follows the same native withdrawal/reconciliation path. If this final reserve-refund transfer itself is uncredited after the reserve has been released, it too needs a top-up; no extra insurance layer exists.

After the author's acceptance deadline expired, the sponsor recovered the escrow, reconciled its child payout, released the untouched reserve, and reconciled a second child payout. ## Adversarial coverage and remaining boundary

The [v0.4 direct tests](../tests/direct/test_reserve_v04.py) cover lost-value and returned-value uncredited children, exact parent/child binding, project segregation, reserve exhaustion, donor replenishment, and one-time release. The [StudioNet integration test](../tests/integration/test_v04_studionet.py) deploys and reads back the actual GenVM code and policy. **No real external `value_credited=false` child was induced on StudioNet.** The two live children above both have `value_credited=true`. The direct-mode negative cases are tests of contract logic, not finalized onchain evidence for that branch. The StudioNet EVM interaction limit and other [evidence boundaries](FAILED_TRANSFER_BOUNDARY.md) remain disclosed. Do not describe this release as production insurance or claim that the negative path was demonstrated live.
